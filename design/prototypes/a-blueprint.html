Questions

The things publishers ask first.

Why does scanning happen on a phone and not my desktop?

The capture step needs a camera you can hold over a page, plus a live guide that tells you when a shot is too dark, too bright, blurred or shadowed. A phone has both. On desktop you get a QR code that hands the session over to your phone; everything after capture — payment, receipt, delivery — is fine on either.

How good is the text recognition?

Clean, well-lit body text in Slovene or English comes through reliably. Heavily set display type, tables, footnotes and marginalia are where OCR gets rough — so review anything typographically unusual before you publish it. The original page images stay in the file, so nothing is lost.

Do I need to remove page numbers and running heads?

Keep them outside the guide frame when you shoot. The guide is sized so that framing the text block alone is the natural thing to do, which keeps headers and folios out of the recognised text.

Who is responsible for the rights?

You are. Pergament is a conversion tool, not a licence — only scan books you hold the rights to, or that are in the public domain.

What happens to my images?

They're processed, turned into your EPUB, and deleted when the seven-day download window closes. There are no accounts, so there is no library of past scans to leak.
