Good to know

Before you scan your first title.

Why the phone and not my desktop?

Capture needs a camera you can hold over a page plus a live guide that catches bad shots. A phone has both. Open the site on desktop and you'll get a QR code that hands the session to your phone; payment and delivery work anywhere.

How accurate is the text recognition?

Clean, evenly lit body text in Slovene or English comes through reliably. Display type, tables, footnotes and marginalia are where OCR struggles — review anything typographically unusual before you publish. The page images stay in the file either way.

What about page numbers and running heads?

Frame just the text block inside the guide and they stay out of the shot — which keeps folios and headers out of the recognised text.

Can I stop halfway and come back?

Yes. A session is kept on your phone, so if you put it down mid-book you'll be offered the option to carry on where you left off.

Who is responsible for the rights?

You are. Pergament converts files; it doesn't grant licences. Only scan books you hold the rights to, or that are in the public domain.
